Frequently asked questions

Gigwatt places electricians and other skilled trades on data center and energy builds. Employers pay $2,500 per hire; workers never pay.

What is Gigwatt?

Gigwatt is an AI-run recruiting company for skilled trades on data center, energy and industrial builds. AI agents find contractors who are hiring, source and screen electricians, low-voltage techs and pipefitters by text, and send the contractor interview-ready introductions. A person reviews every introduction.

How much does it cost to hire through Gigwatt?

Gigwatt charges a flat $2,500 per hire, invoiced after the worker has been on site for 14 days and due in 15 days. There is no upfront fee, no contract and nothing to pay if you don't hire.

Is Gigwatt free for electricians and other tradespeople?

Yes. Workers never pay. Employers pay the placement fee, and a worker's profile is only shared with an employer after the worker says yes to that specific job.

Which trades does Gigwatt place?

Mainly electricians (apprentice, journeyman, foreman and superintendent), low-voltage and structured-cabling techs, fiber splicers, pipefitters and steamfitters, and instrumentation and controls techs, plus millwrights, welders and ironworkers for the same projects.

Where does Gigwatt operate?

Across the United States, with a focus on data center and energy hubs: Northern Virginia (Ashburn, Manassas, Richmond), Texas (Dallas, Abilene, San Antonio), Ohio (Columbus, New Albany), Phoenix, Atlanta, Council Bluffs and Reno.

How fast can Gigwatt find workers?

Sourcing starts the minute a request arrives, and the goal is the first screened candidates within 72 hours. Speed depends on the trade, the location and whether the pay meets what local workers are asking.

How is Gigwatt different from a staffing agency?

Traditional agencies typically charge 15-25% of first-year pay for a direct hire, or a 30-60% markup on temporary labor. Gigwatt charges a flat $2,500 per hire, the worker joins your payroll, and there's no contract to negotiate for the first hire.

What happens if a hire doesn't work out?

If a placed worker leaves within 30 days of the start date, Gigwatt finds a replacement for free. If the invoice hasn't gone out yet, nothing is billed for that hire; if it has, it is credited.

How does Gigwatt screen candidates?

By text: trade and level, where they live and whether they travel, their minimum hourly rate, licences and certifications, and when they can start. Credentials are self-reported, so the employer verifies them before hiring. Each introduction explains why the candidate was matched.

Does AI make hiring decisions at Gigwatt?

No. AI helps read messages, ask screening questions and estimate fit from job-relevant facts only; names, contact details and protected characteristics are removed before scoring. The employer makes every hiring decision.

How do workers get matched to jobs?

Sign up at gigwatt.co/work or text us your trade, city and minimum rate. Gigwatt only texts you when a job fits your trade, area and pay floor. Reply YES to be introduced, NO to skip, or STOP at any time.

Does Gigwatt work with union contractors?

Most Gigwatt employers are merit-shop (open-shop) electrical and mechanical subcontractors. On union jobs, workers are usually dispatched through the local hall instead.
