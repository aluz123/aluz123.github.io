How to hire electricians for a data center project

Short answer: electricians are the scarcest trade on data center builds, so the fastest route is to go where they already are (job boards, traveler networks, other contractors' crews) with a pay rate that beats their current job, screen quickly by text, and decide within days. Gigwatt does that for a flat $2,500 per hire.

Why it's hard right now

  • Data center construction spending hit a record $50.7 billion seasonally adjusted annual rate in April 2026 (U.S. Census Bureau data, via ThinkSet/BRG).
  • Electrical work typically takes 45-70% of a data center's construction budget, so electricians are the bottleneck.
  • Estimates of the 2026 US construction labor gap range from about 349,000 to 499,000 workers, and a licensed electrician takes 4-5 years to train.
  • Data center craft workers averaged about $81,800 a year in early 2026, roughly 32% more than on comparable commercial builds (Skillit), so they move for pay.

What to put in a job request

  1. Trade, level and headcount: "8 journeyman electricians and 1 foreman."
  2. Location and travel: the site's city and state, and whether travelers are welcome.
  3. Pay and per diem: an hourly range plus any daily per diem. Posts with real numbers get far more replies.
  4. Requirements: licences and certifications such as OSHA 30, NFPA 70E or a state journeyman licence.
  5. Start date and duration: "start Nov 17, 6+ months."
