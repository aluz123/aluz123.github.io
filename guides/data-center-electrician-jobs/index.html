Data center electrician jobs: pay, travel and how to get hired

Short answer: data center projects pay electricians more than most commercial work, often with per diem for travelers, and contractors are short-handed in Virginia, Texas, Ohio, Arizona and Georgia. Tell Gigwatt your trade, city and minimum rate once and we text you only when a job beats it. It's free.

What the jobs pay

Data center craft workers averaged about $81,800 a year in early 2026, roughly 32% more than on comparable commercial builds (Skillit). Pay depends on level, state and whether you travel. Check current hourly rates from real job posts on our pay pages, for example electricians in Virginia.

Where the work is

  • Northern Virginia: Ashburn, Manassas, Richmond
  • Texas: Dallas, Abilene, San Antonio
  • Ohio: Columbus, New Albany
  • Arizona: Phoenix
  • Georgia: Atlanta
  • Iowa and Nevada: Council Bluffs, Reno

What employers ask for

  • Level and years: journeyman and foreman roles are the most in demand; apprentices get hired too.
  • Safety cards: OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 show up on most posts we see; NFPA 70E for energized work.
  • Low-voltage and fiber: structured cabling experience, BICSI credentials and fiber splicing.
  • A state licence where the work is, and a willingness to travel for traveler roles.
